PolyFX Shaders

Realistic shading for PolyTrack, ambient occlusion, bloom, tone mapping, god rays, screen-space reflections, a day/night sky, real headlights and brake lights, and underglow all as opt-in graphics presets layered on top of the stock look.

Presets

  • Off: stock rendering.
  • Very Low: tone mapping + a light colour grade only. For integrated GPUs.
  • Balanced: adds subtle bloom and anti-aliasing. The safe default.
  • Enhanced: adds a reflection/IBL map.
  • Semi-Real: adds god rays.
  • Photoreal (Ultra): the full stack, tuned further.

Also in Settings

  • Time of Day: Default (matches the stock sky), or pick a time for a full day/night cycle with a moon and stars.
  • Ambient Occlusion: off by default — it's the single most expensive effect in the mod, worth opting into deliberately rather than paying for it at every preset.
  • Underglow: an emissive strip and road glow under the car, off by default.
  • Glowing Blocks: master on/off here; open the in-game tuning panel (L) to pick any combination of 13 track elements — warning signs, checkpoints, start/finish, track edges, walls, road — each with its own color. Off by default.
  • Auto Perf Guard: on by default — see below.

Runs on low-end hardware

A software-rasterizer (no real GPU) is detected and the mod disables itself automatically. An adaptive performance guard watches frame time and degrades effects one at a time under sustained load, then recovers the same way once there's headroom again — it can reduce quality, but it will never disable the mod outright.

In-game controls

Registered as real PolyModLoader keybinds (category PolyFX), so they show up in — and can be rebound from — PML's own keybind menu:

  • L: open the live tuning panel (every effect, exposed as sliders).
  • F2: free-fly photo mode.
  • F9: save a screenshot (while in photo mode).
