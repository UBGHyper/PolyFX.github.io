PolyFX Shaders

Realistic shading for PolyTrack — ambient occlusion, bloom, tone mapping, god rays, screen-space reflections, a day/night sky, real headlights and brake lights, and underglow — as opt-in graphics presets layered on top of the stock look.

Presets

  • Off — stock rendering, byte-for-byte. PolyFX never touches the scene at this tier.
  • Very Low — tone mapping + a light colour grade only. For integrated GPUs.
  • Balanced — adds ambient occlusion. The safe default.
  • Enhanced — adds bloom, SMAA, and a reflection/IBL map.
  • Semi-Real — adds god rays.
  • Photoreal (Ultra) — adds screen-space reflections.

Also in Settings

  • Time of Day — Default (matches the stock sky), or pick a time for a full day/night atmosphere with a moon and stars.
  • Underglow — an emissive strip and road glow under the car, off by default.

Runs on low-end hardware too

A software-rasterizer (no real GPU) is detected and the mod disables itself automatically. An adaptive performance guard watches frame time and degrades effects one at a time under sustained load, then recovers the same way once there's headroom again — it never gets stuck worse than it needs to be.

In-game controls

  • ` (backtick) — open the live tuning panel (every effect, exposed as sliders).
  • F2 — free-fly photo mode.
  • F9 — save a screenshot (while in photo mode).
